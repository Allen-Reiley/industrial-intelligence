# Design Specification 02: Haute Luxury Editorial Monograph
**Design Code:** `AW-ARCH-LUXURY-MONOGRAPH`  
**Target Sub-Sectors:** Haute Bespoke Furniture Houses, Architectural Bronze & Stone Ateliers, Private Client Studios  
**Archetype & Format:** Museum-Grade Editorial Art Monograph & Private Salon Dossier (Zero generic landing page structures)  
**Aesthetic References:** Bottega Veneta, Rimowa, Hermès, Architectural Digest, luxury Swiss horlogerie publications  
**Color Palette:** Velvet Black (`#050507`), Salon Charcoal (`#0d0e12`), Champagne Bronze (`#dfba73`), Deep Burnished Gold (`#b38938`), Silk White (`#fcfbfa`), Muted Taupe (`#8c92a0`), Hairline Gold (`rgba(223, 186, 115, 0.22)`)

---

## 1. Executive Trust & Psychological Architecture

### 1.1 The Audience & Mindset
* **Target Decision Maker:** Creative Director, Founder, or Propriétaire of an ultra-luxury bespoke design maison (AOV R45,000 to R450,000+).
* **The Reality:** They despise corporate SaaS clutter, cartoon illustrations, flashing badges, and standard ecommerce "Add to Cart" carts. To them, good design is quiet, confident, and deeply refined.
* **The Hook:** When they open Design 02, it does not look like software. It reads like a limited-edition architectural monograph or art publication: wide margins, oversized classical serif typography (*Cormorant Garamond*), editorial chapter divisions, quiet pulled quotes, and an interactive **Private Salon Commission Dossier**.
* **The Commercial Revelation:** It proves that digital technology does not cheapen luxury pricing power. Rather, it introduces white-glove digital provenance, eliminates 20+ hours of manual founder back-and-forth over WhatsApp, and secures a mandatory **50% Private Bank Commitment Deposit** before rare timber flitches or cast bronze plates are cut.

## 3. Key Bespoke Interactive Features

### 3.1 The Private Commission Salon Dossier
* A curated step-by-step bespoke atelier salon:
  1. **Specimen Selection:** Rare wood flitches, hand-rubbed organic oil lusters, and cold-cast bronze hardware.
  2. **Architectural Span Controls:** Length and width sliders styled with champagne gold hairline rules and elegant serif millimeter numerals.
  3. **Formal Acquisition Statement:** Computes live surface area ($m^2$), mass, 4–6 week workshop bench reservation, and **50% Private Commission Deposit Gate**:
     `50% UPFRONT COMMITMENT: R XX,XXX`.
     *"Confirms raw flitch reserve, issues formal Certificate of Provenance, and locks atelier bench calendar."*

### 3.2 Digital Certificate of Provenance Generator
* Interactive preview showing how Auraworks generates an archival Certificate of Provenance for every client commission, displaying log harvest location, artisan signature, timber moisture content, and serial number.

### 3.3 Concierge Admission Application
* Elegant intake modal set in velvet black and hairline champagne bronze, screening applicants against strict capacity limits (1–3 accounts / month) and AOV thresholds (R15k–R450k+).

## 4. Typography & Visual Rules
* **Serif Display:** `Cormorant Garamond` (Google Fonts: 500, 600, 700 with italic accents) — commanding classical elegance.
* **Architectural Labels:** `Montserrat` (Google Fonts: 400, 500) — uppercase, letter-spaced (`0.18em` to `0.25em`).
* **Understated Body:** `Inter` (Google Fonts: 300, 400) — light, airy, and refined.
* **Palette:** Deep velvet black (`#050507`), champagne bronze (`#dfba73`), and silk white (`#fcfbfa`).
