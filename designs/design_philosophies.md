## Detailed Section-by-Section Divergence Guarantee

To ensure **ZERO repetition of layout, components, or user flows**, every single design will have its own unique layout architecture:

1. **Design 01 (CAD Drafting Board):** No hero section. Starts immediately inside a full-screen interactive viewport with coordinate rulers, isometric grid, and dockable CAD tools.
2. **Design 02 (Haute Luxury Monograph):** Full-bleed magazine editorial cover, multi-column narrative chapters, pull quotes, full-page specimen blocks, step-by-step salon dossier.
3. **Design 03 (Factory OS):** Full-screen operating system desktop with draggable windows, live telemetry bar, shop floor Gantt chart, and machine bay queue.
4. **Design 04 (Swiss Broadsheet):** Dense newspaper gazette grid, bold dividing borders, modular multi-column text, mechanical toggle switches, and typographic ledger.
5. **Design 05 (Sensory Materials Lab):** Horizontal material swatch carousel, live texture lighting inspector, physical sample box curation funnel.
6. **Design 06 (Executive Memo):** Formal tabbed memorandum, sensitivity matrix tables, cash-flow waterfall charts, binding commercial term sheet.
7. **Design 07 (Specifier Split):** 50/50 split-screen layout with sticky left portfolio gallery and right-side technical data sheets and BIM file vault.
8. **Design 08 (Scrollytelling Provenance):** Full-screen narrative journey following a single monumental build through 5 production milestones.
9. **Design 09 (B2B Trade Portal):** High-density spreadsheet-like table with keyboard navigation, line-item calculator, wholesale margin toggles, and instant BOQ export.
10. **Design 10 (Brutalist Forge):** Raw industrial brutalism, exposed framework lines, heavy metal fabrication parameters, laser piercing math.
11. **Design 11 (Nordic Sanctuary):** Organic curves, soft pill geometry, humane studio quote builder, and automated client reassurance timeline.
12. **Design 12 (Hypertext Vault):** Interactive file tree drawer, technical drawing archive, and multi-product commercial tender bundler.

* Fiduciary single-order payback guarantee (1 closed commission per month covers the retainer).
* Strict capacity constraint: **1–3 Retained Accounts / Month**.
