STAGE 01 CLIENT FACING

Visual Couch Studio

Customers choose modular sofa segments (3-seater, chaise, U-shape), pick velvet/linen fabrics, and see dynamic lead-time estimates.

STAGE 02 PAYMENT GATE

The customer pays a 50% deposit right on the website. High-intent buyers are locked in immediately; window-shoppers are filtered out.

✓ 100% funded before production
STAGE 03 FACTORY FLOOR

Live Workshop Queue
