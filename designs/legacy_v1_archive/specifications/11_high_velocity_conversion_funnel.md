# Design Specification 11: High-Velocity Deal Room & Conversion Funnel (Commercial Engine)
**Design Code:** `AW-DSGN-11-DEALROOM`  
**Target Sub-Sectors:** High-Ticket Commercial Manufacturers, High-Growth Fabrication Brands, Bespoke Outfitting Studios  
**Strategic Archetype:** Conversion-Optimized Executive Deal Room & Multi-Step Qualification Funnel  
**Primary Psychological Objective:** Rapid qualification, radical financial transparency, frictionless commercial conversion, and zero wasted sales time.

---

## 1. Executive Persona & Trust Thesis

### 1.1 The Prospective Client Profile
* **Target Decision Maker:** Founder, Head of Sales, Commercial Director, or Managing Director of an established physical manufacturing brand or high-ticket fabrication studio.
* **Economic Reality:**
  - Average Order Value (AOV) is **R15,000 to R250,000+** per order.
  - The business receives 30 to 100+ inbound inquiries per month across WhatsApp, email, contact forms, and social media.
* **The Emotional & Commercial Reality:**
  - They are commercially sharp and time-poor. They know that revenue growth depends on sales cycle velocity and lead qualification.
  - **Their Major Pain Point:** Up to 70% of their inbound inquiries are unqualified noise: retail buyers looking for mass-produced products under R5,000, dropshippers wanting cheap overseas margins, or hobbyists asking for small one-off favors.
  - Sifting through this volume manually takes 20+ hours a week of their sales team's time, delaying responses to legitimate commercial architects and high-intent buyers with R100k+ budgets.
  - **What They Need to See:** A high-velocity deal room that automatically qualifies buyers in 60 seconds, demonstrates undeniable financial payback through transparent unit economics, and channels qualified decision-makers directly into a confirmed discovery call.

### 1.2 The Trust Architecture: Why They Trust Auraworks Instantly
1. **Interactive 60-Second Qualification Funnel:** Placed prominently in the hero section, the interactive quiz screens prospects in real time against the commercial brief's criteria (AOV R15k+, physical workshop footprint, manual quoting bottlenecks).
2. **Radical Commercial Transparency:** No hidden fees or ambiguous scopes. The Two-Phase Commercial Architecture (Phase 1: R45k–R75k build; Phase 2: R10k–R18k/mo retainer) is laid out openly alongside an interactive payback calculator.
3. **Mathematical Payback Proof:** Shows workshop owners that because physical goods operate on high gross margins, **just 1 additional closed order per month** completely covers the retainer fee, converting the web platform into an immediate profit generator.

## 2. Bespoke Ecommerce Architecture (Conversion-Focused Storefront)

### 2.1 Visual Atmosphere & Art Direction
* **Canvas Styling:** Deep enterprise slate (`#0f172a`), elevated container panels (`#1e293b`), card surfaces (`#243044`), and electric emerald accents (`#10b981`).
* **Visual Rhythm:** High-contrast text hierarchy, assertive call-to-action buttons, structured multi-step wizard cards, and interactive financial sliders.
* **Typography:**
  - Headings & CTAs: *Plus Jakarta Sans* (Google Fonts: 600, 700, 800) — commanding, clean, and conversion-optimized.
  - Analytical Data & Sliders: *Inter* (Google Fonts: 400, 500, 600) — high-contrast numerical legibility.

### 2.2 The 60-Second Workshop Qualification Funnel
Integrated directly into the hero section is a **Multi-Step Dynamic Qualification Engine**:
* **Step 1 // Deal Economics & AOV Screening:**
  - *Under R5,000 per order:* Instantly displays the disqualification panel, gracefully explaining that custom technical architecture requires high-ticket unit economics.
  - *R15,000 – R45,000 per order:* Unlocks Step 2 (Qualified AOV).
  - *R45,000 – R150,000+ per order:* Unlocks Step 2 (High-Ticket Bespoke).
* **Step 2 // Physical Manufacturing Footprint:**
  - *Solo hobbyist or home garage:* Routes to disqualification panel (no commercial team/machinery).
  - *Established workshop, showroom, or dedicated team:* Unlocks Step 3 (Verified production footprint).
* **Step 3 // Quoting & Intake Bottleneck:**
  - *Unstructured WhatsApp & email chains:* Confirmed fit.
  - *Showroom paper intake forms or manual PDF drawing takeoffs:* Confirmed fit.
* **Instant Step 4 // Verified Qualification Status:**
  - Displays: `VERIFIED QUALIFICATION STATUS: 100% FIT`.
  - Explains how their specific AOV and physical workshop will achieve rapid ROI.
  - Unlocks immediate booking for a 20-minute architecture discovery session.

## 3. Custom Operational Tool Architecture (Back-of-House Systems)

### 3.1 Dynamic Retainer Payback & Unit Economics Simulator
The design includes an interactive financial payback tool that models the client's manufacturing economics:
* **Interactive Parameters:**
  - *Average Deal Size (AOV):* Slider from R15,000 to R150,000 (default: R45,000).
  - *Estimated Gross Margin %:* Slider from 30% to 65% (default: 45%).
* **Real-Time Financial Outputs:**
  - Gross Profit Generated per Closed Order: `R20,250`
  - AuraWorks Growth Retainer (Phase 2): `R14,000 / mo`
  - Net Profit After Retainer on Just 1 Deal: `+R6,250 / mo`
  - Break-Even Metric: Prominently displays `EXACTLY 1 CLOSED DEAL`.

### 3.2 Lead Scoring & Automated Routing Engine
The back-of-house operational tool automates lead triage for the sales team:
* Scores incoming inquiries based on dimensional parameters, material tier, and commercial intent.
* High-ticket commercial specifications (R50k+) are instantly routed to the founder or senior estimator via priority SMS/WhatsApp alert with pre-filled parameter specs.
* Filters out low-value inquiries automatically, saving 15–20 hours of manual sales intake every week.

### 5.2 Typography System
* **Display & Headings:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Google Fonts: 600, 700, 800)
  - Hero H1: `clamp(38px, 5.5vw, 66px)`, weight: `800`, line-height: `1.1`, tracking: `-0.02em`
  - Section H2: `clamp(28px, 4vw, 44px)`, weight: `800`, tracking: `-0.01em`
  - Card Titles: `22px to 26px`, weight: `800`
  - Price Outputs: `32px to 36px`, weight: `800`
* **Analytical Copy & Sliders:** `Inter` (Google Fonts: 400, 500, 600)
  - Lead Copy: `18px`, line-height: `1.6`, color: `#94a3b8`
  - Standard Body: `13.5px to 14.5px`, line-height: `1.55`
  - Step Labels: `12px to 13px`, weight: `700`

### 5.3 Micro-Interactions & Animation Specs
1. **Quiz Step Transitions:** Clicking an option hides the current step and smoothly reveals the next step (`fadeIn 0.25s ease`) without page reload.
2. **Dynamic Payback Sliders:** Dragging the deal size or margin slider recalculates gross profit, retainer coverage, and net profit live with zero lag.
3. **Button Hover Elevation:** 2px vertical lift with radiant emerald glow (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`).
