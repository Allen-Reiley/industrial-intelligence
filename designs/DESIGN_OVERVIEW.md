# AuraWorks Design Suite: 6 Visual & Strategic Directions
**Theme:** Outcome-Driven Digital Systems for Businesses to Make Money & Run Efficiently
**Target:** Product-based businesses & Service-based businesses (Custom manufacturers, specialized workshops, trade & professional services)
**Rule:** Strictly NO pricing displayed anywhere. Plain-English clarity for non-technical executives.

---

## The 6 Distinct Archetypes

### 1. `01_interactive_solutions_workbench.html` — The Interactive Solutions Workbench
- **Visual Personality:** Modern product-led engineering (Linear / Stripe / Vercel meets workshop clarity). Dark slate (`#090D16`), deep navy cards (`#111827`), electric cyan (`#06B6D4`) & amber indicators (`#F59E0B`).
- **Core Narrative:** "Software built around how your business makes money."
- **Hero & Flow:** Split hero featuring a live interactive demonstration:
  - Toggle between "The Manual Nightmare" (WhatsApp screenshots, messy Excel spreadsheets, lost client requests) vs "The AuraWorks System" (clean digital storefront + live workshop dashboard).
  - **Live Interactive Module:** An interactive 3D/SVG Custom Couch Configurator linked to a live Factory Floor Production Queue. When the user changes fabric from "Oatmeal Bouclé" to "Emerald Velvet" or toggles from "3-Seater" to "L-Shape Modular", the adjacent workshop ticket dynamically updates cutting dimensions, fabric yardage, and production bay status.
  - Interactive Admin Time Waste Calculator: Sliders for team members and weekly hours spent quoting/coordinating.
  - Plain-spoken quote request modal with smart requirement tags.

---

### 2. `02_clean_editorial_executive.html` — The Clean Editorial & High-Trust Executive Studio
- **Visual Personality:** Warm minimalist editorial (Monocle / Kinfolk / Apple Enterprise). Warm sand canvas (`#FAF8F5`), crisp porcelain cards (`#FFFFFF`), rich espresso charcoal text (`#1C1917`), subtle warm bronze border accents (`#C88A58`), serif headings (`Fraunces` / `Playfair Display`) paired with clean sans body (`Inter`).
- **Core Narrative:** "We build the custom tools your business wishes existed off the shelf."
- **Hero & Flow:**
  - Asymmetric broadsheet editorial layout with generous breathing room.
  - **Featured Case Study Monograph:** In-depth visual walkthrough of the Custom Couch Manufacturer:
    1. *The Bottleneck:* Answering 40 unstructured WhatsApp messages a day, manual pricing mistakes, lost deposits.
    2. *The Custom Solution:* An intuitive online couch builder where buyers choose layouts, modular segments, and premium fabrics + a live production dashboard for the workshop team.
    3. *The Commercial Impact:* Faster sales cycles, zero missed orders, 15+ hours saved weekly.
  - Dual pillars: "Product Businesses (Custom goods, made-to-order manufacturing, wholesale)" & "Service Businesses (Specialized contractors, consulting, field operations)".
  - "Common Symptoms We Cure" expandable accordion of everyday business headaches.
  - Elegant consultation scheduler card.

### 3. `03_split_screen_dual_engine.html` — The Dual Engine: Front Stage (Sales) & Back Stage (Ops)
- **Visual Personality:** High-concept 50/50 dual-pane architecture. Left side: "Front Stage / Revenue Engine" in deep obsidian (`#0F1115`) with emerald accents (`#10B981`); Right side: "Back Stage / Operational Engine" in crisp light bone (`#F8F9FA`) with dark slate typography (`#0F172A`).
- **Core Narrative:** "A great website shouldn't just look pretty. It should sell for you and run your operations."
- **Hero & Flow:**
  - Dynamic interactive split view:
    - Left side showcases the **Customer-Facing Platform**: Custom couch visualizer, fabric selection, instant deposit capture, automated customer notifications.
    - Right side showcases the **Internal Workshop Dashboard**: Job queue, cut-list generation, upholstery bay assignments, automated supplier alerts.
  - Interactive "Slide to Balance": Users can drag a divider or click toggles to inspect how every customer action directly powers an automated business process.
  - "Who We Build For" matrix comparing physical products vs service businesses.
  - Plain English 3-step engagement blueprint: "1. We Learn Your Business", "2. We Build Your System", "3. We Train Your Team & Launch".
  - Full-width slide-over quote inquiry drawer.

### 4. `04_tactile_bento_showcase.html` — The Tactile Bento Box & Dynamic Metric Lab
- **Visual Personality:** Contemporary Bento Grid / Modern Software Suite. Crisp light mode with subtle layered glass cards, soft drop shadows, deep indigo accents (`#4F46E5`), emerald badges (`#059669`), and micro-interactive widgets.
- **Core Narrative:** "Custom digital tools that turn business chaos into smooth growth."
- **Hero & Flow:**
  - Prominent interactive Bento Grid featuring living preview widgets:
    - *Tile 1:* Live Couch Swatch & Configurator Widget.
    - *Tile 2:* Live Workshop Order Progress Tracker (Cutting → Foaming → Stitching → Ready for Delivery).
    - *Tile 3:* Automated Deposit & Quoting Assistant.
    - *Tile 4:* WhatsApp & Email Lead Ingestion Stream.
  - Business Type Segmenter: Toggle between "We Sell Products (Couches, furniture, equipment, goods)" and "We Sell Services (Installations, custom contracting, specialized trades)" to see tailored workflows.
  - Real-world business outcomes: "No more lost quotes", "Fewer phone calls asking 'where is my order?'", "Predictable weekly production".
  - Interactive Scope & Needs Checklist leading directly to a booking CTA.

### 5. `05_bold_swiss_poster.html` — The Bold Neo-Brutalist & Swiss Poster Studio
- **Visual Personality:** Bold International Typographic Style (Swiss design / high-impact poster). Stark contrast: High-contrast deep black (`#0A0A0A`), crisp stark white (`#FFFFFF`), energetic international safety orange (`#FF4F00`). Thick architectural grid borders (2px solid), monumental typography (`Space Grotesk` & `Plus Jakarta Sans`), high-readability iconography.
- **Core Narrative:** "YOUR BUSINESS IS UNIQUE. YOUR SOFTWARE SHOULDN'T BE A SPREADSHEET."
- **Hero & Flow:**
  - Massive, unapologetic statement headlines that immediately grab business owners.
  - The "Anti-Frustration Manifesto": Calling out why generic SaaS tools (Shopify alone, generic CRMs, endless spreadsheets) fail custom businesses.
  - The 4-Step Couch Case Study Pipeline shown as an industrial visual assembly line with interactive step inspect.
  - "Before AuraWorks vs After AuraWorks" high-contrast checklist.
  - Step-by-step Interactive Project Intake Form with 3 simple non-technical questions to get a customized proposal.

### 6. `06_nordic_minimal_clarity.html` — The Nordic Operational Sanctuary
- **Visual Personality:** Calming Scandinavian Modernism / Industrial Elegance. Soft stone canvas (`#F5F5F3`), deep forest slate (`#1C2826`), muted sage green (`#4A6B5B`), refined serif accents, warm photography styling.
- **Core Narrative:** "Calm operations. Predictable revenue."
- **Hero & Flow:**
  - Warm, human-first message addressing owner burnout and operational friction.
  - The "Story of a Transformation": How a custom couch manufacturer went from working late nights answering WhatsApp quotes to having an automated store and a calm workshop dashboard.
  - "Built for Real People, Not Tech Experts": Explaining how AuraWorks designs software that even the least tech-savvy staff member can pick up in 10 minutes.
  - Interactive "Find the Friction in Your Business" diagnostic: Select your daily pain point and see the exact tool AuraWorks builds to fix it.
  - Low-pressure, conversational call-booking widget with direct founder phone/WhatsApp and calendar integration.
