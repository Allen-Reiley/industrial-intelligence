Case Profile 01 • Verified Client Story

How a Custom Couch Studio Scaled Production by 35%

Made-to-Order Furniture • Cape Town

“We were spending 3 hours every day sending fabric photos on WhatsApp and calculating couch lengths on scrap paper. Our workshop was overwhelmed by mistakes.”

The Growing Business Bottleneck:

The studio crafts bespoke modular couches with custom lengths, depths, and imported linen/velvet fabrics. Because every couch was made-to-order, customers couldn't purchase directly online. The founder was inundated with over 40 WhatsApp messages daily, leading to quote delays of up to 48 hours, invoicing errors, and workshop carpenters misinterpreting order notes.

What AuraWorks Built:

We engineered an intuitive online couch studio where buyers select their layout (3-seater, chaise sectional, U-modular), choose from 24 premium fabrics with real-time renders, and configure custom leg finishes. The system calculates exact pricing and collects an upfront 50% deposit.

Concurrently, the order automatically dispatches to factory tablets. Timber cut-lists, foam density requirements, and fabric yardage are assigned to Bay 2 with zero manual data entry.

The Commercial Outcome:

Quoting time dropped from 48 hours to 30 seconds. Invoicing errors fell to zero, and the founder saved 20+ hours of administrative work each week—allowing the workshop to manufacture and deliver 35% more couches every month.

“For the first time in three years, I can take Sundays off with my family. The website sells for us while we sleep, and the workshop runs without me having to answer 50 phone calls a day.” — Managing Director, Custom Couch Studio
