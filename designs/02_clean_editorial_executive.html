In-Depth Case Profile

Transforming a Custom Couch Studio

Cape Town • Made-to-Order Furniture

“We were spending 3 hours a day sending fabric photos on WhatsApp and calculating custom couch lengths on scrap paper. Our workshop was overwhelmed by mistakes.”

1. The Operational Bottleneck

The furniture studio creates handcrafted couches with bespoke dimensions, imported linens, and modular configurations. Because every piece was custom, customers couldn't purchase directly online. The founder was inundated with over 40 WhatsApp messages daily, leading to missed inquiries, calculation errors on invoices, and delayed production starts.

2. The Bespoke Solution We Built

AuraWorks designed an intuitive online couch studio. Buyers can select their preferred layout (3-seater, L-shape, corner modular), choose from 24 premium fabrics with real-time photo renders, and pick timber leg stains. The system instantly calculates the exact price and collects a 50% deposit.

Simultaneously, the order feeds into an internal factory floor tablet dashboard. The timber frame cut-list and fabric yardage requirements are generated automatically and assigned to Bay 2 for production.

3. The Tangible Results

Within the first 60 days, quoting time went from 48 hours to instant checkout. The company eliminated quote errors completely and saved 20+ hours of administrative work each week—allowing the team to craft and deliver 35% more couches every month.
